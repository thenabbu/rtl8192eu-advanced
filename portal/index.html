Access Point

Station (client)

Join an existing network. Adapter switches mode first — any AP you're running on it stops.

Monitor (sniff)

Captures every frame in the air around the antenna — beacons, probes, handshakes, data. Nothing is sent; the radio only listens.

Mode switching brings the radio down for a couple of seconds and changes what this adapter does on your network. Nothing here touches the driver or kernel — it's a separate process you can kill any time.
